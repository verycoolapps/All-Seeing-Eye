# Photography Intelligence

## Purpose
Analyze photographs with professional eye — composition, exposure, focus, lighting, color, and the technical decisions that separate snapshots from photographs.

## Core knowledge
- **Exposure triangle:** aperture (depth of field, light), shutter speed (motion blur, light), ISO (sensitivity, noise); correct exposure = histogram balanced, no clipped highlights/shadows unless intentional.
- **Composition frameworks:** rule of thirds, golden ratio, leading lines, framing (within frame), symmetry, negative space, rule of odds (3 subjects), fill the frame, depth layering.
- **Depth of field:** shallow (f/1.4-2.8, background blur = subject isolation, portraits/products), deep (f/8-16, everything sharp = landscapes/architecture); bokeh quality (smooth vs. busy circles).
- **Focus accuracy:** critical focus on intended subject; focus stacking for macro; back-button focus technique; missed focus = soft subject with sharp background (error).
- **Lighting in photos:** natural (golden hour, overcast, midday harsh), artificial (flash, continuous, mixed); direction (front flat, side dimensional, backlit silhouette/rim); quality (hard flash vs. soft window).
- **Color in photography:** white balance (daylight 5500K, shade 7500K, tungsten 3200K); color grading (warm/cool, faded, vibrant); color harmony (complementary, analogous, split-complementary).
- **Genre conventions:** portrait (85mm, shallow DOF, eye contact), landscape (wide angle, deep DOF, golden hour), street (35mm/50mm, decisive moment, environmental context), product (controlled lighting, multiple angles), architecture (tilt-shift, correct verticals, HDR).
- **Technical quality:** sharpness (diffraction limits at small apertures), noise (high ISO grain), dynamic range (highlight/shadow recovery), lens distortion (wide-angle stretching, vignette).

## Evaluation criteria / rules
- Evaluate in order: composition → exposure → focus → lighting → color → technical quality → genre appropriateness.
- Check horizon level (unless intentional), verticals straight (architecture), subject sharpness, exposure balance.
- Assess whether technical choices serve the subject: shallow DOF for portraits, deep for landscapes, motion blur for action.
- Flag common errors: clipped highlights (blown sky), crushed shadows (detail loss), soft focus on wrong element, tilted horizon (unless Dutch angle), distracting background elements.
- Consider the "decisive moment": does the photo capture peak action/expression, or is it static documentation?
- Evaluate color: is white balance correct? Does grading serve mood or look like Instagram filter?

## Confidence & limits
- High confidence: exposure errors (blown/crushed), focus misses, horizon tilt, obvious composition violations.
- Medium: "good composition," "professional quality," genre appropriateness.
- Cannot assert: camera settings used, photographer intent, whether post-processing was excessive without before/after.
- Always distinguish technical quality (sharp, well-exposed) from artistic quality (composition, moment, emotion).
